## what makes a good UI

### user classification

half truth = a good UI is the one that is appropriate for it's target audience

market segmentation - narrowing down a user group to determine what kind of UI we need to make

expert or novice user? in what?
-  familiarity with the app - computer skills and general OS knowledge
-  use of a phone - software/app knowledge
-  knowledge of a specific topic - particular domain knowledge

**use user classification table for your projects (computer skill, knowledge of the app, domain knowledge)**

-  we focus on users that are classified as good in the computer skill column when designing UI

**think about who are your users**

## general guidelines

some design is not about preference: readers read faster dark text on light background colour

-  be careful about the contrast between text and background colour
-  use quiet background
-  sharpen/prioritise the information
